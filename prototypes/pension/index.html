Assumptions & sources

Payment amounts

Early payments stay reduced after 65. The reduction percentage shown on each card is permanent. Pensionable service stays fixed after departure.

The temporary bridge normally ends at 65, or earlier for CPP/QPP disability entitlement.

Calculation assumptions

Whole-year approximation. Current salary is a highest-five average proxy; AMPE uses . Amounts are rounded.

No salary growth, indexing, CPP/QPP/OAS, taxes or other income is included. Exact dates, part-time work, buybacks, special service rules, disability and survivor benefits are outside this estimate.
