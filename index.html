Assumptions & sources

Payment amounts

Early payments stay reduced after 65. The reduction percentage shown on each card is permanent. Pensionable service stays fixed after departure.

The temporary bridge normally ends on the first day of the month after your 65th birthday, or earlier for CPP/QPP disability entitlement.

Calculation assumptions

Whole-year approximation. Current salary is a highest-five average proxy; estimated AMPE uses , the 2026 CPP YMPE as a proxy. Actual AMPE averages the retirement year and four preceding years. Amounts are rounded.

No salary growth, future YMPE changes, indexing, CPP/QPP/OAS, taxes, insurance or other income is included. Exact dates, part-time work, buybacks, special service rules, disability and survivor benefits are outside this estimate.
