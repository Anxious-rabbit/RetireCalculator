Sources and assumptions

What this estimate uses

Your current annual salary stands in for your highest-paid five-year average. The estimated AMPE is $74,600, using the 2026 CPP YMPE as a proxy. Actual AMPE averages the retirement year and four preceding years.

This website uses whole years. Official pension calculations may use exact dates, service to the nearest tenth of a year, salary records, and other plan details.

The bridge benefit normally ends on the first day of the month after your 65th birthday, or earlier if you become entitled to CPP/QPP disability benefits. This tool does not calculate disability benefits.

What is not included

No salary growth, future YMPE changes, indexing, CPP/QPP/OAS, taxes, insurance, or other retirement income is included. Buybacks, part-time work, special service rules, complex leave, disability, survivor benefits, and exact contribution dates are outside this estimate.

Actual pensionable service, your plan group, available options, and payments must be confirmed with official records.
